## About

I'm a **BBA student** who likes to test theory against reality. Business is about people, decisions and problems that need solving, and technology is one of the best tools for solving them.

I'm especially curious about entrepreneurship, AI, automation and data analysis, and how they can make a business faster, smarter and easier to run. This profile is where I put that curiosity to work.
